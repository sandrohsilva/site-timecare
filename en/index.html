Your Data Stays on Your Smartphone

Unlike legacy apps that store all records on third-party cloud servers, TimeCare Lite adopts the modern Local-First architecture:

Financial Sovereignty

Your prices, cash intake, and expense records reside exclusively on your local SQLite storage.

Transient Cloud Relay

Cloud infrastructure acts only as a temporary mailbox for bookings; messages are purged immediately upon delivery.

Anti-Spam Quarantine

Incoming public requests enter a private quarantine queue. Accept, reject, or block spam with one tap.

Direct WhatsApp Integration

No clunky in-app chatbots. Confirmations launch directly into verified WhatsApp chats.
